=====================================================================
  AEROX LITE FOR MAC - STEP BY STEP GUIDE
  For the SteelSeries Aerox 3 Wireless mouse
=====================================================================

Aerox Lite is a small free program that replaces SteelSeries GG.
It lets you change the mouse sensitivity, lighting, buttons and more,
and shows the battery level in the menu bar.

This download is for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 ...).
To check: Apple menu  -> About This Mac -> "Chip: Apple M..."


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1. QUIT STEELSERIES GG (IMPORTANT)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

SteelSeries GG fights with Aerox Lite over the mouse settings.

  1. Click the SteelSeries GG icon in the menu bar (top right) and
     choose Quit.
  2. Optional but recommended: Apple menu -> System Settings ->
     General -> Login Items, select SteelSeries GG and click "-".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2. INSTALL THE APP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Double-click AeroxLite-macos-arm64.zip to unzip it
     (Safari may already have done this for you).
  2. Drag "Aerox Lite" into the Applications folder.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The app is free and not registered with Apple, so macOS asks once.

  1. Open the Applications folder.
  2. RIGHT-click (or Control-click) "Aerox Lite" and choose "Open".
  3. Click "Open" in the warning window.

  If macOS only offers "Move to Trash" / "Done":
    - Click "Done".
    - Apple menu -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
    - Scroll down. Next to "Aerox Lite was blocked", click
      "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password.

  After this, it opens normally with a double-click.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4. CONNECT THE MOUSE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Use either:
    - the wireless USB dongle (2.4 GHz), or
    - the USB cable.
  (A USB-C adapter is fine.)

  Turn the mouse on (switch on the bottom set to 2.4 GHz).

  In the Aerox Lite window, at the top right, you should see
  something like:  "● 2.4 GHz · 85%"
  That means the mouse is connected and shows its battery level.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5. CHANGE YOUR SETTINGS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The window has three tabs in the middle: Settings, Lighting, Modules.
Changes are applied to the mouse right away.

  SETTINGS tab
    - SENSITIVITY LEVELS (CPI): click a level (LV. 1, LV. 2 ...) and
      move the slider. "+" adds a level, "Remove level" deletes one.
      The CPI button behind the scroll wheel switches between levels.
    - POWER SAVING: when the mouse goes to sleep and when the
      lighting dims.
    - POLLING RATE: 1000 Hz is the smoothest, 500 Hz saves battery.

  LIGHTING tab
    - LIGHTING MODE: Static, Off, Rainbow, Breathing, Wave ...
      Modes marked with * only work while Aerox Lite is running.
    - ZONES: click a zone to pick its color.
    - OPTIONS: brightness and effect speed.

  BUTTONS (left side, "ACTIONS")
    - Click the box next to a button and choose what it should do:
      another button, a keyboard key, a media key, or a macro.
    - "RESET BINDINGS" puts everything back to normal.

  >>> When you are happy, click "SAVE TO MOUSE" (bottom right). <<<
  This stores the settings inside the mouse, so they keep working
  on any computer, even without Aerox Lite.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6. EVERYDAY USE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Closing the window does NOT quit the app. It keeps running in
    the menu bar (top right) and shows the battery level there.
  - Click the Dock icon or the menu bar icon -> "Open" to bring the
    window back.
  - To quit completely: menu bar icon -> "Quit", the "QUIT" button
    at the top of the window, or press Cmd + Q.
  - Tick "Start on login" (bottom left) to start it automatically.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
EXTRAS (OPTIONAL) - NEED TWO PERMISSIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Macros, multi-click and smart lighting need permission to see and
send mouse and keyboard input. Settings and lighting work without it.

  1. Apple menu -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
  2. Open "Input Monitoring" and switch Aerox Lite ON.
     (If it is not in the list, click "+" and choose it from
     Applications.)
  3. Go back, open "Accessibility" and switch Aerox Lite ON.
  4. Quit Aerox Lite (Cmd + Q) and open it again.

  MACROS - one button does a sequence of keys
    1. Under ACTIONS, next to "Macro editor", click "OPEN".
    2. Click "+ NEW", type the steps, click "SAVE".
       The help on the right shows what you can type, for example:
           cmd+c
           wait 50
           cmd+v
    3. Close the editor. Click the box next to a mouse button and
       choose Macros -> your macro.
    4. Click "SAVE TO MOUSE".
    Up to 8 macros. They only work while Aerox Lite is running.

  MODULES tab
    - Multi-click: a quick press of the left or right button sends
      2-6 clicks. Holding the button works normally.
      Note: on a Mac this also affects trackpad clicks.
    - Smart battery: shows how many hours are left and saves battery
      automatically when it drops below 25%.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  "mouse not found"
    - Make sure the mouse is switched on and the dongle is plugged in.
    - Unplug the dongle, wait 5 seconds, plug it back in.
    - Try another USB port, adapter, or the cable.
    - Make sure SteelSeries GG is quit (step 1).

  Macros or multi-click do nothing
    - Check both permissions (EXTRAS above), then restart the app.
    - If the permissions are already ON, switch them OFF and ON again
      (macOS sometimes forgets them after an update of the app).

  "Aerox Lite is damaged and can't be opened"
    - Open Terminal and paste this line, then press Enter:
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Aerox Lite.app"
      Then open the app again.

  Settings are lost after restarting the computer
    - You forgot to click "SAVE TO MOUSE".

  Still stuck?
    - Open a new issue on the GitHub page of this project and attach
      the log file:
          ~/Library/Application Support/AeroxLite/log.txt
      (Finder -> Go -> Go to Folder..., paste the path).
