=====================================================================
  AEROX LITE FOR MAC - STEP BY STEP GUIDE
  For the SteelSeries Aerox 3 Wireless (and other SteelSeries mice)
=====================================================================

Aerox Lite is a small free program that replaces SteelSeries GG.
It lets you change the mouse sensitivity, lighting, buttons and more,
and shows the battery level in the menu bar.

Two ways to install - pick one:
  A) Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, M4 ...): the ready-made disk image
     AeroxLite-macos-arm64.dmg from the Releases page.
  B) Any Mac, including Intel: the automatic installer
     "Install Aerox Lite.command" from the source download.
To check your Mac: Apple menu -> About This Mac -> "Chip" (Apple M...)
or "Processor" (Intel).


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1. QUIT STEELSERIES GG (IMPORTANT)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

SteelSeries GG fights with Aerox Lite over the mouse settings.

  1. Click the SteelSeries GG icon in the menu bar (top right) and
     choose Quit.
  2. Optional but recommended: Apple menu -> System Settings ->
     General -> Login Items, select SteelSeries GG and click "-".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2. INSTALL THE APP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  A) DISK IMAGE (Apple Silicon)
    1. Double-click AeroxLite-macos-arm64.dmg.
    2. In the window that opens, drag "Aerox Lite" onto "Applications".

  B) AUTOMATIC INSTALLER (any Mac, including Intel)
    1. On the GitHub page click "Code" -> "Download ZIP", then
       double-click the zip to unzip it.
    2. RIGHT-click "Install Aerox Lite.command" -> "Open" -> "Open".
       (Right-click is needed only the first time.)
    3. A Terminal window opens and shows every step. Answer its
       questions with Enter (= yes):
         - install Python if it is missing (asks your Mac password;
           nothing appears while typing, that is normal)
         - quit SteelSeries GG if it is running
    4. It builds the app (a few minutes) and then opens a window:
       drag "Aerox Lite" onto "Applications".
    5. It starts Aerox Lite for you. Continue with STEP 3, item 4.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The app is free and not registered with Apple, so macOS asks once
(only for the disk image; the installer's own build is not blocked).

  1. Open the Applications folder.
  2. RIGHT-click (or Control-click) "Aerox Lite" and choose "Open".
  3. Click "Open" in the warning window.

  If macOS only offers "Move to Trash" / "Done":
    - Click "Done".
    - Apple menu -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
    - Scroll down. Next to "Aerox Lite was blocked", click
      "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password.

  4. Aerox Lite now asks for two permissions in macOS dialogs:
       - Input Monitoring ("receive keystrokes")
       - Accessibility
     In each one click "Open System Settings" and switch Aerox Lite ON.
     Then quit Aerox Lite (Cmd + Q) and open it again.
     Needed for macros, multi-click and smart lighting only.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4. CONNECT THE MOUSE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Use either:
    - the wireless USB dongle (2.4 GHz), or
    - the USB cable.
  (A USB-C adapter is fine.)

  Turn the mouse on (switch on the bottom set to 2.4 GHz).

  In the Aerox Lite window, at the top right, you should see
  something like:  "● 2.4 GHz · 85%"
  That means the mouse is connected and shows its battery level.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5. CHANGE YOUR SETTINGS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The window has three tabs in the middle: Settings, Lighting, Modules.
Changes are applied to the mouse right away.

  SETTINGS tab
    - SENSITIVITY LEVELS (CPI): click a level (LV. 1, LV. 2 ...) and
      move the slider. "+" adds a level, "Remove level" deletes one.
      The CPI button behind the scroll wheel switches between levels.
    - POWER SAVING: when the mouse goes to sleep and when the
      lighting dims.
    - POLLING RATE: 1000 Hz is the smoothest, 500 Hz saves battery.

  LIGHTING tab
    - LIGHTING MODE: Static, Off, Rainbow, Breathing, Wave ...
      Modes marked with * only work while Aerox Lite is running.
    - ZONES: click a zone to pick its color.
    - OPTIONS: brightness and effect speed.

  BUTTONS (left side)
    - Click the box next to a button and choose what it should do:
      another button, a keyboard key, a media key, or a macro.
    - "Reset" puts everything back to normal.

  ANOTHER STEELSERIES MOUSE (Rival, Sensei, Prime, Aerox 5/9, ...)
    - Just plug it in. Aerox Lite recognises 65 more SteelSeries models by
      itself; there is nothing extra to download.
    - The window then looks like the Aerox 3 one, made for that mouse:
      a picture of it, its buttons on the left (choose another
      button, a key, a media key or a macro for each), and the tabs
      Settings (sensitivity, polling rate; battery and sleep timer on
      a wireless mouse), Lighting (each colour zone) and Modules.
      Changes apply at once; "Save to mouse" keeps them inside the mouse.
    - The model name appears under "Aerox Lite" at the top left.

  LIGHT OR DARK
    - The switch at the top left (moon / sun) changes between the dark
      and the light look. Your choice is remembered.

  WINDOW SIZE
    - Text and buttons too small or too big? Use "A-" and "A+" at the
      top right of the window (50% to 200%). The size is remembered.

  >>> When you are happy, click "Save to mouse" (bottom right). <<<
  This stores the settings inside the mouse, so they keep working
  on any computer, even without Aerox Lite.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6. EVERYDAY USE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Closing the window does NOT quit the app. It keeps running in
    the menu bar (top right) and shows the battery level there.
  - Click the Dock icon or the menu bar icon -> "Open" to bring the
    window back.
  - To quit completely: menu bar icon -> "Quit", the power button
    at the top of the window, or press Cmd + Q.
  - Tick "Start on login" (bottom left) to start it automatically.
  - To remove Aerox Lite: double-click "Uninstall Aerox Lite.command".
    It asks what to remove: 1) only the app, or 2) everything - the app,
    its settings and the downloaded folder, including the installer
    and the uninstaller.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
EXTRAS (OPTIONAL) - NEED TWO PERMISSIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Macros, multi-click and smart lighting need permission to see and
send mouse and keyboard input. Settings and lighting work without it.
Aerox Lite asks for them itself (STEP 3). If you skipped the dialogs:

  1. Apple menu -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
  2. Open "Input Monitoring" and switch Aerox Lite ON.
     (If it is not in the list, click "+" and choose it from
     Applications.)
  3. Go back, open "Accessibility" and switch Aerox Lite ON.
  4. Quit Aerox Lite (Cmd + Q) and open it again.

  MACROS - one button does a sequence of keys
    1. In BUTTONS (left side), click "Macros...".
    2. Click "+ NEW", type the steps, click "SAVE".
       The help on the right shows what you can type, for example:
           cmd+c
           wait 50
           cmd+v
    3. Close the editor. Click the box next to a mouse button and
       choose Macros -> your macro.
    4. Click "Save to mouse".
    Up to 8 macros. They only work while Aerox Lite is running.

  MODULES tab
    - Multi-click: a quick press of the left or right button sends
      2-6 clicks. Holding the button works normally.
      Note: on a Mac this also affects trackpad clicks.
    - Smart battery: shows how many hours are left and saves battery
      automatically when it drops below 25%.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATING
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Launchpad / Applications -> "Update Aerox Lite".
  It checks GitHub for a newer version, backs up your settings, replaces
  Aerox Lite in Applications and starts it again. Your settings, macros
  and lighting are kept. On an Intel Mac it builds the new version, like
  the installer did.
  The first time, "Update Aerox Lite" is not there yet: double-click
  "Update Aerox Lite.command" (on the disk image or in the source
  download) once, allowing it like the installer. It then adds
  "Update Aerox Lite" to Applications.
  Permissions: the first update asks for your Mac password once, to set
  up a lasting signature for Aerox Lite. macOS then asks once more for
  Input Monitoring and Accessibility; after that they are kept across
  all updates.
  Disk space: the updater itself is tiny. On an Intel Mac it keeps a
  build folder of roughly 150-300 MB in ~/Library/Caches/AeroxLite-updater
  so the next update is faster; you may delete it at any time.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  "...could not be executed because you do not have appropriate
  access privileges" when opening Install Aerox Lite.command
    - The file lost its "executable" flag while being copied.
      Open Terminal, type  cd  and a space, drag the unzipped folder
      into the Terminal window, press Enter, then paste:
          chmod +x *.command *.sh
      Now double-click the installer again. Or start it directly:
          bash "Install Aerox Lite.command"

  "mouse not found"
    - Make sure the mouse is switched on and the dongle is plugged in.
    - Unplug the dongle, wait 5 seconds, plug it back in.
    - Try another USB port, adapter, or the cable.
    - Make sure SteelSeries GG is quit (step 1).

  Macros or multi-click do nothing
    - Check both permissions (EXTRAS above), then restart the app.
    - If the permissions are already ON, switch them OFF and ON again
      (macOS sometimes forgets them after an update of the app).

  "Aerox Lite is damaged and can't be opened"
    - Open Terminal and paste this line, then press Enter:
          xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Aerox Lite.app"
      Then open the app again.

  Settings are lost after restarting the computer
    - You forgot to click "Save to mouse".

  Still stuck?
    - Open a new issue on the GitHub page of this project and attach
      the log file:
          ~/Library/Application Support/AeroxLite/log.txt
      (Finder -> Go -> Go to Folder..., paste the path).
